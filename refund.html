Refund Policy

We want every ISP operator to test ISPConnect with full confidence before paying. Please review how refunds work for Cloud subscriptions, prepaid wallets, Enterprise installations, and hotspot end-users.

1. ISPConnect Cloud Subscriptions

  • Every new Cloud tenant receives a 14-day free trial to verify MikroTik compatibility, M-Pesa payments, and portal performance prior to subscribing.
  • Because full platform access is provisioned immediately upon payment, monthly subscription fees (KES 1,300/month) are non-refundable once activated for the billing cycle.
  • You may stop renewing at any time with no cancellation penalty.

2. Prepaid SMS & Payment Wallet Top-Ups

  • SMS credits and Payment Wallet top-ups are non-refundable once credited to your tenant wallet, though unused balances remain in your account for future use while active.
  • If a verified duplicate M-Pesa payment occurs due to a billing gateway error, contact us within 7 days with the M-Pesa receipt code so we can credit or refund the duplicate charge after verification.

3. Enterprise Edition (Full System Ownership)

Enterprise licensing, custom development, and server deployment milestones are governed by the agreed project quote. Once source code handover or server deployment has commenced, setup and license payments are non-refundable.

4. Wi-Fi Hotspot End-Users

ISPConnect provides billing software to independent local Wi-Fi operators (ISPs). Payments made for Wi-Fi packages go directly to the specific ISP operating that hotspot. If you paid for Wi-Fi access and did not connect, please use the Support Phone Number displayed on that hotspot's captive portal to request reconnection or a refund directly from your Wi-Fi provider.

5. Contact for Billing Disputes

For Cloud or Enterprise billing issues, email support@ispconnect.co.ke or WhatsApp +254 712 153 539 with your account email and M-Pesa transaction reference.
