Privacy Policy

ISPConnect ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal data in accordance with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard data across our SaaS platform and captive portals.

1. Information We Collect

  • ISP Account Holders (Tenants): Business name, contact email, support phone number, MikroTik router identifiers, payment gateway configuration (e.g., PayHero channel IDs), and billing/wallet history.
  • Hotspot End-Users: Mobile phone numbers used for M-Pesa STK Push and SMS voucher delivery, device MAC addresses, local IP addresses, M-Pesa transaction receipts, and active session timestamps.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate Wi-Fi hotspot sessions via FreeRADIUS and MikroTik routers.
  • To verify M-Pesa payments, generate access vouchers, and send transactional SMS notifications.
  • To provide ISPs with real-time analytics, revenue reporting, router heartbeat monitoring, and technical support.
  • To prevent fraud, unauthorized voucher sharing, and abuse of network resources.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell personal data. We share only the minimum data necessary with trusted infrastructure and payment partners strictly to deliver the service, including M-Pesa/PayHero payment processors, SMS gateways, and cloud database/hosting providers.

4. Data Security & Retention

We use encrypted HTTPS connections, role-based access controls, and secure database storage to protect account and session records. Session and transaction logs are retained only as long as required for billing reconciliation, customer reconnection, and statutory compliance.

5. Your Rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, subject to legal and financial record-keeping obligations. Hotspot end-users should first contact their local Wi-Fi provider (ISP), or reach us directly.

6. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries or data requests, contact us at support@ispconnect.co.ke or call/WhatsApp +254 712 153 539.
